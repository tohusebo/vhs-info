Privacy in VHS

Publisher: Tommy Husebø, Norway. Contact: zerocool_angela@protonmail.com.
Last updated: 11 November 2026. Applies to VHS on iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Mac.

VHS connects to the Jellyfin server you choose. The server address, user information and sign-in token are used for authentication, your library and playback. The server login is stored in Apple Keychain on your device. Your password is not retained after sign-in. Media, images and subtitles may be retrieved from the server and the addresses it supplies. Playback progress, watched status and favourites are sent to your server. The server operator decides how server data is retained and deleted. Connection security also depends on the server address and setup you choose.

App preferences, profile information and cached media data are processed locally. The login remains until you sign out, replace it or remove it through your device controls. Preferences and caches may remain after sign-out. Signing out does not delete your server account or viewing history. Ask the server operator for access, correction or deletion of server data. VHS does not create a separate online account for you.

If you enable iCloud sync, selected profile preferences are synced through your Apple account. Sign-in tokens and passwords are not part of this preference sync. Phone pairing transfers encrypted login information between your devices on the local network. The publisher does not run a separate sign-in or viewing-history service for these features and does not receive an additional copy of your login or history through them. Turning sync off stops further syncing; it does not guarantee deletion of copies already held by Apple.

When ratings and reviews are retrieved, Trakt receives public IMDb, TMDB or Trakt title IDs and normal network information, including your IP address. Jellyfin credentials, your private server address and viewing history are not sent to Trakt. Responses are cached on your device. Your media server, the image/media services it uses and Trakt may keep request logs. Their own terms, retention periods and international-transfer rules apply. VHS no longer uses an external Streamed connection or a weather service.

YouTube may receive an image request when a trailer preview is shown. Official trailer links open with YouTube. YouTube and Google terms and privacy policies then apply. VHS does not download or extract YouTube video for playback inside the app.

The app contains no advertising or tracking SDK, and no separate usage or viewing-history analytics service has been added. Apple may process TestFlight feedback, screenshots, crashes and diagnostics and share them with the publisher. If you email or send test feedback, the message and contact information are used to answer your request and investigate problems. Do not include passwords, tokens or private media in feedback. Support information is kept while the case is handled and afterwards only as needed for follow-up, documented defects or legal obligations. Request deletion when the information is no longer needed.

Contact the publisher about VHS, support requests or personal information processed by the publisher. You may request access, correction, deletion and other rights available under applicable law. Data held by your chosen server, Apple, Trakt or Google also needs to be handled with the relevant data holder. You can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority or your local supervisory authority.

The privacy and license pages are hosted on GitHub Pages once published. GitHub may retain your IP address in security logs when you visit these pages. The pages contain no analytics tools, sign-in forms or external tracking scripts. The publisher relies on a legitimate interest in answering support requests and investigating defects when handling support cases. Ask your server operator or other service providers about their own grounds for processing and retention.
